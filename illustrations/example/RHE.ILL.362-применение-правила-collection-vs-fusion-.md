# [RHE.ILL.362] Применение правила collection-vs-fusion к реальной административной иерархии; поддержка геометрического примера

## Source

Consider the states of the USA and the counties of the USA. If we see the counties or the states classes as a fusion of their individual extensions, the result is a single extension—the USA. However, if we see them as collections of their member's extensions, we get two separate collections of extensions.
