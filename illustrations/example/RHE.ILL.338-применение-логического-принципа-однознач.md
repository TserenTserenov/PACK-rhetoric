# [RHE.ILL.338] применение логического принципа однозначности в конкретной модельной системе

## Source

It is that a sign (concept) in the business model explicitly refers to one and only one object in the business. And conversely a business object is explicitly referred to by one and only one sign in the model. This principle, if strictly adhered to, ensures that a business model is a direct, and so explicit, reflection of the world.
