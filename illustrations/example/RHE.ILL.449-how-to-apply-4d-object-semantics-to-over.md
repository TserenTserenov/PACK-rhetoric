# [RHE.ILL.449] How to apply 4D-object semantics to overcome the classical paradox of change (how something can have contradictory properties at different times while remaining identical)

## Source

"If my car was red last week and green this week, then we need to start 
instinctively seeing a red temporal part (stage) followed by a green temporal 
part (stage), where the temporal parts are time-slices of the whole car."

## Structural Core

Changes in surface properties can be reinterpreted as a sequence of temporal stages (time-slices) of a single 4D object, preserving identity through change without paradox

## Boundaries (breaks_when)

when the object undergoes substantial loss or complete transformation (e.g., a car crushed into scrap metal) rather than surface-property change, making temporal-slice interpretation unintuitive
