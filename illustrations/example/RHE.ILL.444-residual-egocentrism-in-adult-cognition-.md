# [RHE.ILL.444] Residual egocentrism in adult cognition; psychological evidence that temporal egocentrism is deep and hard to overcome

## Source

Most of us half believe in a variation of Murphy's law—that things happen when they 
are most inconvenient for us. So, we half suspect that it is raining because we forgot 
our umbrella or because we were going out to play tennis. Whereas, 'objectively' we 
know that the weather is not influenced by our future plans.

## Structural Core

Egocentric bias: attributing objective physical events to one's own temporal position/plans; persistence even when conscious knowledge contradicts it

## Boundaries (breaks_when)

When there is genuine causal coupling between agent's plans and environmental outcome (rare but possible)
