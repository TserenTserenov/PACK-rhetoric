# [RHE.ILL.348] Как ум активно конструирует восприятие реальности, выходя за границы сенсорных данных

## Source

Objects have a certain coherence even as they appear to our sense; but this coherence is much greater and more uniform, if we suppose the objects to have a continu'd existence; and as the mind is once in the train of observing an uniformity among objects, it naturally continues, till it renders the uniformity as compleat as possible.
