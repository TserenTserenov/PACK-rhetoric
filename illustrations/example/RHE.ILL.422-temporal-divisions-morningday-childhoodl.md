# [RHE.ILL.422] Temporal divisions (morning/day, childhood/life) expose that the logical paradigm's notion of extension, which works for spatial parts, cannot handle parts distributed across time.

## Source

We talk about the morning being part of the day, of our childhood being part of
our life. These whole–part patterns do not fit into the logical paradigm's
extension-based explanation.

## Structural Core

[Temporal whole-part relations] + [extension-based semantics] → [explanation fails for duration/sequence]

## Boundaries (breaks_when)
