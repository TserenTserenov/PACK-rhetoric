# [RHE.ILL.441] Historical precedent for space-time equivalence in human representation; oldest evidence of treating time as readable-like-space

## Source

There is an ancient precedent for this interchanging of time and space, one that we are 
all familiar with—writing. Its characters use space to describe the way speech's sounds 
change over time.

## Structural Core

Writing encodes temporal sequence (phonetic time-order) via spatial sequence (left-to-right positions); prototype space-time translation

## Boundaries (breaks_when)

When symbolic systems do not map temporal order to spatial extension (e.g., musical notation, though even music maps time to score-space)
