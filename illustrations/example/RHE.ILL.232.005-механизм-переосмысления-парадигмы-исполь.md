# [RHE.ILL.232.005] Механизм переосмысления парадигмы: использование знакомого, интуитивно правильного паттерна для коррекции ошибок в менее знакомом контексте

## Source

When we imagine water pushed down a similarly coiled garden hose laid flat on the lawn, everyone knows it gushes straight out. When the thought experiment involves something as familiar as a garden hose, we can predict the results properly. We then use the familiar pattern from the garden hose experiment to correct our pattern for the marble experiment.
