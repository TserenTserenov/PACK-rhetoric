# [RHE.ILL.254.006] Changes are the fourth type of object; they happen to things and differ from things by their transience.

## Source

"A change such as a green tomato turning red. The change (turning red) happens to the tomato (a particular thing). Things persist through time; whereas changes do not, they happen."

## Structural Core

A change is a transformation that affects a particular thing and does not persist; it is distinguished from things by temporal instability.

## Boundaries (breaks_when)

When a change persists indefinitely (becomes a stable state) or when it occurs to nothing (is not anchored to a particular thing).
