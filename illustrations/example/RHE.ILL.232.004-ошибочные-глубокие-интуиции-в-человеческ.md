# [RHE.ILL.232.004] Ошибочные глубокие интуиции в человеческом мышлении, которые сохраняются несмотря на формальное обучение правильным законам

## Source

Imagine a coiled tube laid flat on a table with a marble shot at great speed into the inner end. It would speed around the coils and come out fast at the outer end. The question is: What direction does it go in once it has left the tube? Psychologists found most people (around 70 percent) chose direction 1. This includes physics graduates who have been taught the laws of motion.
