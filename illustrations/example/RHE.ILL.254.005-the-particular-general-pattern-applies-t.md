# [RHE.ILL.254.005] The particular-general pattern applies to relationships as well as to things and types; it is a universal semantic principle.

## Source

"The particular relationship 'one person is the mother of another' can be generalised to a general relationship: the type mother is a mother of the type children."

## Structural Core

Particular relationships can be lifted to general relationships by substituting instances with their types; generalization preserves relation structure.

## Boundaries (breaks_when)

When a particular relationship has no natural generalization (e.g., 'sat on this specific chair on Tuesday') or when moving to types loses semantic content.
