# [RHE.ILL.219] How paradigms trained in one direction inhibit perception of alternative interpretations that should trigger re-engineering

## Source

A psychology experiment shows three boxes. When people see numbers (12–13–14) first, they continue seeing '13' in subsequent boxes, even when shown A–B–C. Reversing the order: when shown letters first, people see 'B' in the middle box and sometimes 12–B–14. What people see first affects what they do not see later.

## Structural Core

First-perceived pattern creates cognitive lock that prevents recognition of alternative patterns, even when original pattern is incomplete

## Boundaries (breaks_when)

When alternative pattern is introduced without context of first pattern, or when conscious effort is made to shift perspectives explicitly
