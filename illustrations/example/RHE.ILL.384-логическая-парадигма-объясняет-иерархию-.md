# [RHE.ILL.384] логическая парадигма объясняет иерархию через отношение включения членов, где substance парадигма не давала объяснения

## Source

In the substance paradigm, secondary substances were arranged in a hierarchy of generality. For instance, animal substance was regarded as more general than pig substance. Things are very different with classes. The class animals is regarded as a super-class of the class pigs, which means that every member of the class pigs is also a member of the class animals.
