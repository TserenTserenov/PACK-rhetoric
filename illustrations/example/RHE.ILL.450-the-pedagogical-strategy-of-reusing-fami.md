# [RHE.ILL.450] The pedagogical strategy of reusing familiar spatial reasoning patterns to bootstrap understanding of unfamiliar temporal patterns

## Source

"For instance, we have no trouble recognising that a steering wheel is a 
(spatial) part of the car. All we need to do is learn to re-use these patterns 
on temporal and spatio-temporal whole–parts."

## Structural Core

Spatial whole-part patterns already intuitive from everyday experience (steering wheel as part of car) serve as scaffolding template for learning less-intuitive temporal and spatio-temporal patterns

## Boundaries (breaks_when)

when temporal parts are treated as physically separable entities like spatial parts are; a temporal time-slice cannot be removed and stored like a wheel, exposing the limits of the analogy
