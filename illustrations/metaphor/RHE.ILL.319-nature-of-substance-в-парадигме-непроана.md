# [RHE.ILL.319] Nature of substance в парадигме: непроанализируемая, однородная, дающая идентичность. Это образное понимание создает ограничения (single classification, static classification).

## Source

Primary substance is conceived as a homogenous hunk of matter underlying things, 
giving a body its identity and continuity over time. This image of substance as 
undifferentiated material substratum shapes how the paradigm handles classification 
and change.
