# [RHE.ILL.297] Столкновение двух несовместимых парадигм объяснения природы материи

## Source

Since the 17th century, scientists have regarded matter as the small particles of which
things are composed. They believe that the way these small particles of matter behave
determines the thing's attributes. So a piece of lead is heavy because its particles are
heavy; a piece of cloth is red because its particles emit light rays of the right wavelength.
Modern scientists' particle matter is very different from Aristotelian substance.
