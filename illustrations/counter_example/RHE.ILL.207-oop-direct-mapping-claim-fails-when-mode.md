# [RHE.ILL.207] OOP direct-mapping claim fails when models impose artificial operations that don't exist in reality

## Source

If you drink a cup of tea, you do not invoke the drink operation on the cup anymore 
than the cup invokes the drink operation on your lips, or, indeed, anything invokes 
an operation on anything else.

## Structural Core

Direct mapping of OOP operations onto real-world actions produces absurd models [cup.drink() vs actual physical action]

## Boundaries (breaks_when)

When domain is highly abstract or conceptual (not physical); or when working with true abstract objects without physical analogues
