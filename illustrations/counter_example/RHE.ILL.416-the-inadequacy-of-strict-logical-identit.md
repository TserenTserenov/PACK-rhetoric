# [RHE.ILL.416] The inadequacy of strict logical identity rules; human perception admits gradual change without registering identity loss, violating the logical rule that demonstrates strict identity destruction

## Source

A change in any considerable part of a body destroys its identity; but where the change is produced gradually and insensibly we are less apt to ascribe to it the same effect. Yet a change in any considerable part destroys identity strictly speaking.

## Structural Core

Logical rule: [Considerable change in body] → [Identity destroyed]. Counter: [Considerable change produced gradually and insensibly] → [Identity NOT perceived as destroyed]. Reveals gap between logical definition and phenomenological experience.
