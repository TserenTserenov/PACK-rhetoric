# [RHE.ILL.226] Structural isomorphism across disciplines: paradigms in different domains operate by the same principle—identification and organization around fundamental units

## Source

There is a close analogy between the way physical matter paradigms work—such as the atom 
paradigm, where particles are fundamental building blocks—and the way information paradigms 
work. Both organize around fundamental particles as foundational units.

## Structural Core

[Physics paradigm: particles = fundamental building blocks] ↔ [Information paradigm: fundamental units structure the domain]; both identify core particles and construct entire frameworks around them
