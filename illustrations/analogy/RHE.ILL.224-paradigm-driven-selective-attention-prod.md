# [RHE.ILL.224] Paradigm-driven selective attention produces recurring structural blindness across different diseases, eras, and scientific domains

## Source

Some scientists suggest that AIDS researchers might be stuck with a 'virus paradigm' 
that directs them to only look for a virus as the cause, potentially making them ignore 
alternative patterns—similar to how the germ paradigm directed researchers in the 
beriberi case to ignore nutritional factors.

## Structural Core

[Disease search in beriberi: germ-paradigm → ignores nutrition] ↔ [Disease search in AIDS: virus-paradigm → ignores alternatives]; both paradigms channel attention and create systematic blindspots

## Boundaries (breaks_when)

when the virus truly is the sole cause (as with many viral diseases) or when alternative causes are demonstrably less fruitful than pursuing viral hypotheses
