# [RHE.ILL.440] Dimensional projection as unified principle: both professions already practice compactification of higher dimensions into lower representational media

## Source

Business modellers compact the four spatio-temporal dimensions onto a two-dimensional 
piece of paper; time is translated into space. This is analogous to the way an architect 
describes the three spatial dimensions of a building on a two-dimensional piece of paper—
compacting three spatial dimensions into two.
