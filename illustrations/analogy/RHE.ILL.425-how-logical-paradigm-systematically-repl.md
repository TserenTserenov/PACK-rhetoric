# [RHE.ILL.425] How logical paradigm systematically replaces substance paradigm's vocabulary while preserving ontological relationships

## Source

In the substance paradigm, a thing was particular if it had primary substance and general if it had secondary substance. In the logical paradigm, a thing is particular because it is an individual extension and general if it is a class of extensions. The notion of primary substance has been replaced by the notion of extension, and the notion of generality by that of class.
