# [RHE.ILL.002] Fundamental particles are civilizational-scale determinants across domains and historical timescales

## Source

A similar analogy can be made with the way in which we talk of early human civilisations. We talk of a Stone Age followed by the Bronze and then the Iron Age. These names refer to the material (the fundamental particles) used to make tools. The nature of the 'particles' clearly had an enormous influence on the overall nature of the civilisation. Advances in material (particles) led to substantial advances in technology.

## Structural Core

Evolution of material type [particles] → evolution of tool technology → evolution of civilization capability; similarly, information paradigm particles determine system capability

## Boundaries (breaks_when)

Civilization possesses advanced material but lacks knowledge systems, cultural transmission, or collective capability to exploit it
