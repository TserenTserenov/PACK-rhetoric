# [RHE.ILL.006] pattern reuse across orthogonal dimensions: same compositional logic applies to time as to space

## Source

You may have noticed that we are treating temporal (time) parts in the same way as we
treat spatial parts. The steering wheel, gear stick and dashboard are all spatial parts of my
car. My car today is a temporal part of my car. Car-minus is a spatio-temporal part of my car.

## Structural Core

spatial whole-part structure (component ⊂ composite) → temporal whole-part structure (time-slice ⊂ 4D object) via structural isomorphism

## Boundaries (breaks_when)

when temporal parts exhibit properties spatial parts lack, e.g., causality directedness, or when temporal mereology has different logical properties than spatial mereology
