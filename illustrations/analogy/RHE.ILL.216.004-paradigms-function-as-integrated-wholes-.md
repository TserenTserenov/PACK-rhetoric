# [RHE.ILL.216.004] Paradigms function as integrated wholes where parts are mutually dependent and meaningless in isolation.

## Source

In a holistic framework, the whole is more than the sum of its parts. For a paradigm, this
means that we do not see its parts until we have seen the whole. In other words, we can
only see the elements that make up a paradigm as its parts in the context of the whole
paradigm.

## Structural Core

In holistic system, constituent elements derive meaning and visibility only within the context of the complete whole (as puzzle pieces derive meaning from complete image, not in isolation).

## Boundaries (breaks_when)

If paradigm elements possessed intrinsic meaning independent of the whole, or if paradigms were decomposable into autonomous, pre-meaningful parts.
