# [RHE.ILL.216.002] Paradigm shifts fundamentally change which questions practitioners regard as worth asking, rendering prior efforts obsolete.

## Source

In the 19th century, chemists assumed that things were made out of indivisible billiard
ball-like atoms. In this scheme, it made sense for chemists to devote a lot of effort into
trying to calculate precisely how much the standard billiard ball atom of a particular element
weighed. When the paradigm for atoms shifted in the 1920s, atoms were seen as miniature
solar systems with divisible parts. Chemists then lost all interest in atomic weights and
stopped trying to calculate them. They regarded all their previous efforts as irrelevant.

## Structural Core

Condition A (atoms as indivisible) + Condition B (goal: calculate atomic weight) → shift to Condition C (atoms as divisible, with isotopes) → result: previous goal rendered meaningless.

## Boundaries (breaks_when)

If isotope discovery had not revealed that atomic weights were mixtures of isotope ratios, or if atomic weight remained useful under new paradigm.
