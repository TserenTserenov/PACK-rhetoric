# [RHE.ILL.222] Paradigm-induced perceptual blindness: practitioners cannot see evidence contradicting their embedded paradigm, even when consequences are fatal

## Source

In the early years of the twentieth century, there was an epidemic of beriberi in Asia 
that killed millions. The medical establishment assumed it was caused by germs, as the 
germ paradigm dictated. Dietary experiments proved the true cause was a vitamin B1 
deficiency in polished rice, not a microorganism.

## Structural Core

[Paradigm: disease = microbe] + [Evidence: disease = nutritional deficiency] → [Paradigm blindness delays diagnosis, causes mass death]

## Boundaries (breaks_when)

when practitioners explicitly question the paradigm or when evidence becomes undeniable and younger generation rejects the old framework
