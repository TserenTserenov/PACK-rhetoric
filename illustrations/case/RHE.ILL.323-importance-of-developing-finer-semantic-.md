# [RHE.ILL.323] importance of developing finer semantic distinctions; why explicit separation of literal and metaphorical represents progress in understanding

## Source

Ancient philosopher describes salt water as the sweat of the Earth, implying sweat and sea-water are the same. Aristotle criticises this, saying metaphors are adequate for poetry but not for understanding the nature of things.

## Structural Core

insufficient semantic framework (no distinction between literal and metaphorical) → metaphor used for definition → confusion about nature of things; adequate framework → clear definition enabling understanding

## Boundaries (breaks_when)

when metaphorical description is explicitly labeled as such and not used for definitions
