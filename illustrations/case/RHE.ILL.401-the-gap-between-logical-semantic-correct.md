# [RHE.ILL.401] The gap between logical-semantic correctness and human intuitive understanding of relationships

## Source

Most people naturally assume that sentences describing different types of relationships about the same individuals (e.g., "A is the parent of B" and "A is taller than B") refer to different connections. They do not naturally assume both refer to the same formal structure. To them, the logical paradigm's more accurate way of seeing is counterintuitive.
