# [RHE.ILL.414] The inadequacy of invariance as a criterion for identity through change; logical definition conflicts with actual human judgment

## Source

Suppose any mass of matter, of which the parts are contiguous and connected, to be placed before us; we must attribute perfect identity if all parts continue unchanged. But suppose some small part to be added or subtracted—this absolutely destroys the identity of the whole, strictly speaking; yet we still pronounce the mass the same where we find trivial alteration.

## Structural Core

[Condition: invariance] → [Identity preserved] BUT [Condition: small addition/subtraction] → [Identity destroyed logically] YET [Observation: human perception accepts it anyway] = Tension between logical and perceptual identity

## Boundaries (breaks_when)
