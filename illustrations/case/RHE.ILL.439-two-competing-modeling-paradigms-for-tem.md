# [RHE.ILL.439] Two competing modeling paradigms for temporal processes: dynamic (kinetic) vs. static (spatial projection)

## Source

An engineer's working model of a steam engine has pistons that move up and down when 
fuel is burnt. A business model does not work this way—it models one process following 
another in time as one process following another across a piece of paper. Business 
modellers compact four spatio-temporal dimensions onto a two-dimensional piece of paper; 
time is translated into space.

## Structural Core

Engineer model represents change through motion; business model represents change through spatial sequence. Both model same 4D process, different representational strategies

## Boundaries (breaks_when)

When internal complexity requires dynamic simulation, or when spatial layout cannot encode temporal dependencies faithfully
